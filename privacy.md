# Privacy Notice

*Last updated: [date]. This notice is written for South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).*

## 1. Who we are

Study Table is a website where students form groups to prepare for exams together. It is run by [your full name or company name] ("we"), the responsible party under POPIA.

Contact: [email address]. Information Officer: [name and email].

## 2. What personal information we collect

- **Account details** from Google sign-in: your name and email address. We use your Google name (or the first part of your email) as your starting display name, which you can change on your Profile page.
- **What you add**: your study goal, posts, replies, likes, reports you make, study materials and files you upload, sessions you schedule, and which sessions you plan to attend.
- **Group activity**: which groups you join, leave or create, and your role in them.
- **Study progress**: the number of focus minutes your timer records.
- **Technical data**: our hosting providers keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) to run and secure the service.

We do not ask for your student number, ID number, phone number, home address or payment details.

## 3. Why we use it

- To create your account and let you use groups, forums, materials and sessions.
- To keep the community safe, including handling reports and removing content that breaks the Community Rules.
- To keep the service secure and working, including limiting how fast an account can post so the service is not abused.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for advertising.

## 4. Who can see what

Study Table is for signed-in students, not the open internet. Anyone with a Study Table account can see your display name, your posts and replies, the groups you belong to, and materials you share. Only members of a group can post or reply in it. Group admins can also see reports made in their group.

Your email address is not shown to other students. Do not put sensitive details about yourself or others (ID numbers, health, addresses) in posts or files.

## 5. Service providers and where data is kept

We use these providers to run Study Table. They are "operators" under POPIA and process data only for us:

- **Supabase**: database, sign-in and file storage. Data is hosted in [region, for example Cape Town or Frankfurt].
- **Vercel**: website hosting.
- **Google**: sign-in.

If any of this data is stored or processed outside South Africa, we do so only where the provider is bound by obligations that give your information a similar level of protection, as POPIA requires for transfers outside the country.

## 6. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account yourself on your Profile page. When you do, we delete your profile, group memberships, likes, replies, uploaded files, scheduled sessions and session RSVPs. Posts that other students have replied to stay in the group so the conversation still makes sense, but their text is replaced and your name is removed. Your other posts are deleted. Backups and provider logs may keep copies for up to [number] days before they expire.

## 7. How we protect it

Access to the database is restricted by rules so that you can only change your own content, and only group members can post in a group. Connections are encrypted. We limit how fast accounts can post to reduce abuse. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we will tell you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

## 8. Your rights

You may ask us to:

- tell you what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy;
- correct information that is wrong or out of date;
- delete your information or your account;
- stop processing your information (object), and withdraw consent you gave.

Email [contact email]. These requests are free. We will reply within [30] days.

If you believe we have mishandled your information and we have not resolved it, you may complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za.

## 9. Age

Study Table is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have, we will delete the account. If you are a parent or guardian and think this has happened, contact us.

## 10. Changes

If we change this notice in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
