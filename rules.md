# Community Rules and Terms of Use

*Last updated: [date].*

## 1. Who can use Study Table

You must be 18 or older and sign in with your own Google account. One person, one account.

## 2. How we expect you to behave

- **Be respectful.** No harassment, threats, hate speech, bullying or discrimination.
- **Keep it about studying.** No spam, advertising, selling answers or tutoring offers in other people's groups without the group admin's agreement.
- **Play fair academically.** Do not share live exam papers, leaked tests, or answers to assessments you are sitting. Do not use Study Table to cheat, to complete someone else's graded work, or to break your institution's academic integrity rules. Study Table is for learning together.
- **Protect privacy.** Do not post other people's personal information or private messages, and do not share screenshots of people without their permission.
- **Respect copyright.** Only upload material you made or have the right to share. Do not upload textbooks, paid course content or past papers that you are not allowed to distribute.
- **Keep it safe.** No malware, no attempts to break into the service or other people's accounts, no automated scraping or flooding.
- **No illegal or explicit content.**

## 3. Reporting and moderation

Use the Report button if you see something that breaks these rules. Group admins review reports for their group and can remove posts, replies and members. We may also remove content or suspend accounts that break these rules, with or without notice where the harm is serious. If you think we got it wrong, email [contact email].

## 4. Your content

You keep ownership of what you post and upload. By posting, you give us permission to store it and show it to other Study Table users as the service is designed to work. You are responsible for what you post and you confirm you have the right to share it.

## 5. Study material is not professional advice

Posts, summaries and guides come from other students. They may be wrong. Check important facts against your lecturer's material and prescribed textbooks. Study Table does not guarantee exam results.

## 6. The service

We run Study Table on a best-effort basis. It may change, be interrupted, or be withdrawn. Keep your own copies of anything important. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for losses that come from using the service or from other users' content. Nothing here limits rights you have under South African consumer or privacy law.

## 7. Ending your account

You can stop using Study Table at any time and delete your account on your Profile page.

## 8. Governing law

These rules are governed by the laws of the Republic of South Africa.
